Why Nayestones

Luxury measured in emotion, not opulence

Nayestones was founded in 2017 by designer Natalie Schayes. Every piece is designed in her studio near Brussels and handmade in Antwerp, the heart of the diamond trade since the 15th century.

We work with recycled 18k gold, re-cut diamonds and lab grown diamonds made with renewable energy. Conscious jewellery, made to last a lifetime.
